Privacy Policy

Animal Rider (애니멀 라이더)

Animal Rider does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. There is no server, no account and no analytics. The game runs entirely on your device.

What the game stores

Your progress and settings are written to your browser's localStorage on the device you play on: stage progress and stars, unlocked animals, audio volume and the interface language. This data never leaves the device. Clearing the site data for this app, or uninstalling it, deletes it permanently - we hold no copy and cannot restore it.

What the game does not do

  • No personal information is requested, collected or stored - no name, e-mail address, phone number or date of birth.
  • No account or sign-in of any kind.
  • No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting or advertising SDK.
  • No cookies and no tracking identifiers.
  • No access to location, camera, microphone, contacts or files.
  • No purchases. The in-game currency and animal gacha are play-money only and cannot be bought.
  • Nothing is sold or shared with third parties, because nothing is collected in the first place.

Network use

The app downloads its own files (page, script, artwork) from its own web address, then caches them so it can run offline. It makes no requests to any other domain. It contains no third-party fonts, no content delivery network and no remote scripts.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and is not directed at collecting data from anyone, including children under 13. Since no data is collected at all, no parental consent mechanism is required.

Changes

If a future version ever collects anything, this page will be updated in the same release and the change stated plainly here.
